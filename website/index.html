GOOD TO KNOW

Little questions.
Straight answers.

Is it really free?+

Yes. The app and its complete MIT-licensed source are free. There’s no paid tier, subscription, account, or paid API to configure.

Does it need a MacBook with a notch?+

No. LensCue also sits at the top center of a display without a notch. The current download is for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14 or newer.

Will it stay hidden when I share my screen?+

Screen-capture invisibility is not guaranteed. Share a specific application window when appropriate, and check what your audience can see.

What if word following isn’t available?+

Word following needs Apple’s on-device English speech model. If it’s unavailable, choose Voice activity or Auto-scroll. LensCue doesn’t fall back to cloud recognition.

Can it guarantee perfect eye contact?+

The camera-adjacent position reduces how far your eyes travel. Reading style, text width, and viewing distance still matter. Use a comfortable narrow width and practice a short take.
